Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I book an appointment?

Open the app, select an available date and time, choose your desired treatment type, and click "Book Appointment". You'll receive immediate confirmation of your appointment.

How do I cancel an appointment?

Open the app, go to "My Appointments", select the appointment you want to cancel, and click "Cancel Appointment". We recommend canceling at least 24 hours in advance.

What happens if I'm late for an appointment?

If you're late for an appointment, please contact us as soon as possible. We'll try to find a solution together. In cases of significant delay, it may be necessary to schedule a new appointment.

How do I update my personal information?

Open the app, go to Settings, and there you can update your personal information.

How do I enable notifications?

Notifications are enabled automatically. If you're not receiving notifications, check your device's notification settings and app permissions.

The app isn't working - what should I do?

Try closing the app completely and reopening it. If the problem persists, try uninstalling and reinstalling the app. If the issue still exists, contact us by email.
