Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Ron Turgeman app. We are committed to protecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal information. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what your rights are.

2. Information Collection

We collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, phone number
  • Appointment Information: Dates, times, treatment types
  • Photos: Photos you upload to the gallery (if you choose to do so)
  • Technical Information: Device ID, IP address, device type

3. Use of Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Managing appointments and bookings
  • Sending notifications and reminders about appointments
  • Improving our service and your experience
  • Communicating with customers
  • Security and fraud prevention

4. Information Security

We use advanced security measures to protect your information:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure authentication system
  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Limited Access: Only authorized employees can access information
  • Secure Backups: Information is backed up regularly

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following cases:

  • When required by law
  • With trusted service providers (such as Firebase) who help us operate the app
  • When necessary to protect our rights or user security

6. User Rights

You have the following rights:

  • Access to Information: You can request to see the personal information we hold
  • Update Information: You can update your personal information at any time
  • Delete Information: You can request to delete your personal information
  • Object to Processing: You can object to processing your information for certain purposes

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our app uses minimal tracking technologies. We use Firebase Analytics for statistics and service improvement only.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with the update date. We recommend checking the policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests regarding the privacy policy, please contact us:

Email: support@turgibarber.com
